# Major equipment material audit — 2026-09-23

This is a preliminary official-source register for AV Portal. The uploaded workbook and supplier request sheet remain local. No product schema, final catalogue, or redistribution right is approved here.

## Coverage

- The workbook has 359 research candidate rows, including 19 new NovaStar and Colorlight options.
- All 294 non-RTCOM candidate rows received a model-led official-source search. The linked register contains 695 URL records for 271 rows. The other 23 rows have no official URL recorded in this pass; that does not prove absence.
- The 65 RTCOM workbook rows use manufacturer-direct material only. No RTCOM web research was done. Twelve of the 28 Portal targets are outside this workbook and remain in the local request appendix.
- A Panasonic PT-VMZ61B front image was visually confirmed at 2500×1253 pixels: https://eu.connect.panasonic.com/sites/default/files/media/image/2024-04/vmz61_b_front_high.jpg . The workbook does not identify the color variant and reuse rights are open. No image is approved for republication.
